# Module 04 — Niche discovery and lead capture

## Objectives
Choose a niche, research local business listings, classify website opportunities, and produce a website requirements brief.

## Workflow
1. Choose a niche and location.
2. Search Google Maps manually and inspect publicly visible business profiles.
3. Record only necessary business-level details and source URLs.
4. Use Instant Data Scraper only on sources where automated extraction is permitted; do not assume Google Maps permits scraping.
5. Clean, deduplicate, and verify collected records.
6. Classify: Tier 1 social-only/no website; Tier 2 website/no online purchasing; Tier 3 outdated website; Tier 4 established ecommerce website.
7. Prioritize leads and draft a business website opportunity report.

## Exercise
Research 20 businesses using compliant collection methods. Do not collect private personal information or send bulk unsolicited messages.

## Deliverables
Lead tracker, niche assessment, research sources, client brief, and presentation slides (to be prepared after walkthrough verification).
